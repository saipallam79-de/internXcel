Questions, answered

A clearer way
to begin.

How long is an internship?

Most paths are designed as a focused 30-day experience with six to eight practical modules.

Do I receive an offer letter?

Yes. After enrollment, InternXcel creates a personalized offer letter using your verified profile and internship data.

How do modules unlock?

Module 1 starts available. Each next module unlocks after the previous task is submitted and approved by an admin reviewer.

When do I receive a certificate?

After all modules, required tasks, and the final project are approved, your certificate and LOR become available.
